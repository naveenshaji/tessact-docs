## Overview

The **General** tab is where admins make the workspace recognizable. It covers the workspace name, logo, and the settings that shape how the workspace appears in admin and across the product.

![Workspace general settings](/images/admin/admin-workspace-general-live-v2.jpg)
</Frame>

---

## Why Identity Matters

Workspace identity is not cosmetic only. Clear naming and recognizable visuals make it easier to:

- distinguish active workspaces from stale ones
- avoid duplicate workspace creation
- keep admin reviews understandable at org scale

---

## Guide: Keep Workspace Identity Clear

<Steps>
<Step title="Review the workspace name first">
  Make sure the title clearly reflects the operating team, initiative, or business function behind the workspace.
</Step>
<Step title="Use a recognizable logo or icon">
  The workspace logo should make the workspace easy to spot in both admin and product surfaces.
</Step>
<Step title="Treat identity updates as governance work">
  Rename or refresh a workspace when the operating model changed enough that the old name is now misleading.
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Best Practices

- Prefer stable, descriptive names over temporary project shorthand.
- Use visuals that help admins and operators distinguish similar workspaces.
- Review workspace identity during quarterly cleanup, not only at creation time.
