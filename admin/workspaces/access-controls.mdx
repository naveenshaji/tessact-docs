## Overview

The **General** tab also includes the workspace-level behavior toggles that affect project visibility and exported media behavior.

---

## Settings In This Section

Current controls include:

- **Project files visibility**
- **Project tasks visibility**
- **Analyze AI clips exports**
- **Enable video watermarking**
- **Custom watermark text** when watermarking is enabled

These settings change how the workspace behaves, not who belongs in it.

---

## Guide: Change Access Behavior Carefully

<Steps>
<Step title="Review project visibility toggles first">
  Use the project visibility controls to decide how much task members can see inside projects in this workspace.
</Step>
<Step title="Change export settings only with a clear reason">
  Export-analysis and watermark settings affect downstream media behavior, so they should be changed intentionally rather than casually.
</Step>
<Step title="Watch enterprise-gated controls">
  Some export settings are gated to enterprise plans. If the toggle is unavailable, check plan context before assuming the setting is broken.
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## When To Use These Controls

Use them when:

- the workspace needs tighter or broader project visibility
- exported media should follow a specific watermark policy
- the workspace has a distinct automation or clip-processing rule

Do not use them as a substitute for user or role cleanup.

---

## Best Practices

- Keep access behavior aligned with the real operating model of the workspace.
- Review visibility toggles whenever external or temporary collaborators are added.
- Document why watermarking or export-analysis behavior differs from other workspaces.
