## Overview

The **Overview** page is the landing surface for Tessact organization admins. It is designed to answer four questions quickly:

- How close is the org to its seat limit?
- How many users are active in the organization?
- How many workspaces need governance?
- How much storage is already consumed?

This page is not where admins make most changes. It is where they decide what needs attention next.

---

## What The Page Shows

The overview surface is built around four cards:

| Card | What it tells you | Why it matters |
|------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Seat capacity** | Used seats versus total allowed seats | Helps you catch onboarding bottlenecks before invites fail |
| **Users** | Total users plus a recent-user preview | Sends you into the user directory for access and activity review |
| **Workspaces** | Total workspace count plus recently created workspaces | Helps you spot growth, sprawl, and ownership issues |
| **Storage usage** | Used storage versus org capacity | Signals when to review workspace limits or cleanup plans |

The **Users** and **Workspaces** cards are also navigation shortcuts into the directory pages where the operational work happens.

---

## How Admins Should Use It

Use the overview as the top of a regular admin review loop:

1. Check **seat capacity** before onboarding or large imports.
2. Open **Users** when access, activity, or role assignments need attention.
3. Open **Workspaces** when the org is creating too many workspaces or leaving old ones unmanaged.
4. Review **storage usage** before teams hit limits or request capacity changes.

This keeps the admin job operational. Tessact expects the overview to be a monitoring surface, not a settings dump.

<Info>
The exact numbers come from live organization data. If a metric looks incomplete, the backend may still be calculating subscription or storage details for that org.
</Info>

---

## What To Check First

For a lightweight weekly review:

- Look for **seat pressure** when the remaining seat count is low.
- Look for **unexpected user growth** in the Users card.
- Look for **workspace sprawl** if the workspace count is climbing without clear ownership.
- Look for **storage acceleration** rather than only absolute usage.

For a monthly governance review:

- Audit inactive or duplicate users.
- Review whether workspaces still have clear owners.
- Confirm storage usage still matches the plan and operating model.

---

## Best Practices

- Treat the overview as a triage surface, not the final destination.
- Use it at the start of onboarding, offboarding, or quarterly access reviews.
- Follow the metric cards into **Users** or **Workspaces** instead of trying to manage exceptions from memory.
- Pair seat and storage reviews with real workflow changes, not only billing discussions.
